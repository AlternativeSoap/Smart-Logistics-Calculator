## ✨ What Can It Do?

### 📦 **Inventory Management**
- **ABC Analysis** - Find your most valuable items instantly
- **Wilson EOQ** - Calculate perfect order quantities
- **Reorder Point** - Know exactly when to order more
- **Periodic Review** - Plan fixed-schedule ordering
- **Min/Max Levels** - Set and track safety stock

### 🏭 **LEAN Manufacturing Tools**
- **OEE Calculator** - Measure equipment effectiveness (with confetti when you hit world-class! 🎉)
- **SMED Savings** - Calculate setup time reduction benefits
- **7 Wastes Analysis** - Identify and cost your waste
- **SWOT Matrix** - Strategic planning (export as image!)
- **Reference Library** - Quick guides for 5S, 7R, 3M, PDCA, JIT, Kanban, FIFO, Kaizen

### 💰 **Budget Management (NEW!)**
- **Bank Account Transfer System** - Track expenses across Budget and Daily Use accounts
- **Smart Transfer Recommendations** - AI-powered suggestions based on historical spending patterns
- **90th Percentile Learning** - System learns from your actual vs budgeted variance
- **Comprehensive Excel Export** - Full budget breakdown with account indicators and transfer recommendations
- **Number Formatting Options** - Danish, US, Space, or Indian formats with currency symbols
- **Monthly Budget Tracking** - Plan 12 months + 14-day periods with visual progress indicators

### 🎨 **Custom Pages**
Build your own calculators with custom formulas - no coding required!
