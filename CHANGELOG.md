# Changelog

A plain-English history of what changed and why.

---

## Version 2.2 — February 2026

### The app is now installable

You can now install Smart Logistics Calculator directly from your browser — no app store, no download needed.

**What's new:**
- A prompt appears on mobile and desktop asking if you want to install the app.
- On Android and desktop Chrome/Edge, tapping **Install** adds it to your home screen or taskbar and it opens in its own window.
- On iOS Safari, the prompt walks you through the three-tap process (Share → Add to Home Screen → Add).
- The app works fully offline after the first load — all files are cached automatically.
- The prompt won't bother you again for 7 days if you dismiss it.

## Version 2.1 — November 2025

### Budget management got a lot smarter

This update was all about making the budget tool genuinely useful for day-to-day use, not just as a planning spreadsheet.

**Two separate accounts**
You can now split every budget row between two accounts — one for fixed bills (rent, subscriptions) and one for your daily spending. This mirrors how most people actually manage money.

**The app learns your spending**
After a few months, the app looks at how your actual spending differed from your budget and uses that history to suggest a safer transfer amount. So if you usually spend more in December, it will account for that automatically.

**Better Excel exports**
Exported spreadsheets now show which account each row belongs to, plus a transfer recommendation section so you can see exactly how much to move between accounts each month.

**Number formats and currencies**
You can now pick how numbers are displayed — Danish style (1.000,00), US style (1,000.00), or others — and set your currency symbol (kr, $, €, £, or custom).

## Version 2.0 — 2025

### A major step up from the original

Version 2.0 added a lot of new tools and made the whole app feel more polished.

**What was added:**

- **Dashboard** — a summary page showing total items, total value and your top 5 items at a glance.
- **Drag and drop uploads** — drag a CSV or Excel file straight onto the page instead of clicking through a file picker.
- **Data quality check** — before you run an analysis, the app scans your data for problems like duplicates, missing values or outliers and tells you what it found.
- **View your data** — scroll through your uploaded data in a table before running anything.
- **Excel export** — download your ABC analysis as a formatted .xlsx file with two sheets (full results + summary).
- **Compare two periods** — upload data from two different time periods and see what changed — which items moved between A, B and C, and how values shifted.
- **Adjustable ABC thresholds** — change where the A/B/C boundaries sit. The default is 80/15/5 (classic Pareto) but you can tighten or loosen it to fit your business.
- **Better EOQ graph** — zoom, pan, hover for exact values, and download the chart as an image.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — Ctrl+U to upload, Alt+A for ABC, Alt+W for Wilson EOQ, Ctrl+E to export, and more.
